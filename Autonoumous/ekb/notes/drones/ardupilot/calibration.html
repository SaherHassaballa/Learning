Sensor Calibration Workflow

Complete this before any first flight or after replacing/moving the flight controller.

1. Accelerometer calibration

Level the vehicle on each of its six faces when prompted by the ground station. This maps the raw accelerometer output to a known gravity vector in each orientation.

2. Compass calibration

Rotate the vehicle slowly through all axes to sample the magnetic field in every direction. Do this away from metal objects, cars, and rebar-reinforced floors.

3. Radio calibration

Move every stick and switch on the transmitter to its full range so the autopilot learns your PWM endpoints and trims.

⚠ Recalibrate the compass any time you change your GPS/compass module's position, or if you see erratic yaw behavior on the bench (spinning heading with the vehicle still).

Pre-flight checklist

  • Accelerometer calibrated on all 6 orientations
  • Compass calibrated away from interference
  • Radio endpoints and trims calibrated
  • Battery voltage/current monitor configured
  • Failsafe behavior set (RTL or land)
